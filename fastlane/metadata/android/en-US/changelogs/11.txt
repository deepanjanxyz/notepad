Elite Memo Pro has been rebuilt from the ground up in Kotlin, with a modern Compose interface and a new modular architecture.

- Write text notes, checklists and freehand drawings
- Organize notes with labels, colors and pins
- Search and filter notes, in grid or list layout
- Archive and Trash, with restore or permanent delete
- Set reminders and get notified on time
- Light, dark or system theme, plus an optional app lock

Thank you for using Elite Memo Pro!
