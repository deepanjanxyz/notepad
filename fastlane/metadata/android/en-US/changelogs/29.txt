Lists, checklists and a format menu

- Bulleted and numbered lists in any note, with no checkboxes.
- A format menu switches between plain text, checklist, bullets and numbers, and marks the one in use.
- Text between items stays editable; the add row shows the marker the new line gets.
- Numbered lists renumber as items are added or removed.
- A Checklist card in the create sheet opens the editor ready to tick items.
- Note cards show bullets as dots.
