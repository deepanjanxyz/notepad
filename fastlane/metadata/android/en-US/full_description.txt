Elite Memo Pro is a minimal, privacy-first notepad for Android.

- Secure Biometric Lock (Fingerprint/Face/PIN)
- Auto-Save: your text is saved automatically as you type
- Timestamps: track when each note was created or modified
- Smart full-text search across titles and content
- Dark Mode & Eye-friendly UI
- 100% Offline: No trackers, no ads, no internet required.
